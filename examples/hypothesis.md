# Examples experiment (simulated traffic, not study data)

This experiment exists only on a local, self-hosted Flagsmith. Every identity that reaches it
runs a simulated task: no model is called, prices are illustrative and no money is spent. Its
numbers say nothing about real routing.

<!-- hypothesis:start -->
Simulated traffic from the examples directory (no model calls, illustrative prices): routing the delegate lane to the small model (lanes_v1) lowers mean task_cost against control, without lowering task_solved. Not study data.
<!-- hypothesis:end -->
