# Code of conduct

This project is an experiment that other people are invited to check. Checking means disagreeing sometimes, and that is welcome here. How it is done matters.

## What we ask

Argue about the method, the data and the code, with evidence. "Step 3 gave me a different Merkle root, here is my output" is the kind of report this repository exists for.

Assume the other person is acting in good faith until they show otherwise. A replication that fails is not an accusation, and a maintainer who asks for more detail is not brushing you off.

Keep it about the work. Insults, harassment, remarks about who someone is, and publishing anyone's private information are not acceptable.

Do not post keys or credentials, yours or anyone else's, even to make a point about a leak. [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) says how to report one.

## Where it applies

Issues, pull requests, discussions and review comments in this repository, and any other place where someone is speaking for the project.

## If something goes wrong

Use the "Report" option in the menu of the comment or issue. Depending on the repository's settings it goes to the maintainers or to GitHub's moderators, and nothing is posted in public.

Maintainers may edit or hide a comment, lock a thread, or block an account from the repository, and will say why. If you think a decision was wrong, say so in a new issue and it will be looked at again.
