# World board: the tolled junction

## Place and people
A motorway junction with an express-lane toll gantry. People: traffic engineers, toll operators, drivers reading signs at speed.

## Visual traditions
1. Guide signs (MUTCD / motorway): green field, white retroreflective legend and border, rounded corners, arrows with fixed geometry; Highway Gothic letterforms; never decorative, never more than 3 lines.
2. Dynamic message signs: black housing, amber LED dot matrix, unlit dots faintly visible; monospaced cells; text centred; numbers right-aligned in price signs.
3. Lane-use control signals: square black heads; green down-arrow, red X, yellow diagonal arrow; one symbol per lane.
4. Road markings: white broken lines (3 m dash / 9 m gap ratio), solid edge lines, chevron gore area at the fork, yellow on left edge.
5. Work-zone devices: orange diamond boards, black legend, striped barricade (orange/white diagonal), cones.
6. Engineering drawings of junctions: plan view, thin lines, lane widths dimensioned, station marks (chainage) like "km 12+400".
7. Toll receipts: thermal paper, monospaced print, lane number, amount, timestamp.

## Physical actions
Split (fork the traffic), merge, close a lane (drop the X), read a gantry at speed, pay a toll, cone off, measure chainage.

Chosen tradition: **motorway guide signs + LED toll gantry**. Chosen action: **split and merge** (Flagsmith's bucketing splits; the rollout merges). No experiment dashboard uses a junction.
